# SoilSim Scientific Methodology

## Overview
SoilSim evaluates crop rotation candidates using historical observed climate data and FAO-56 soil-water balance principles.

## Core Pillars
1. **Hindcasting**: Evaluating candidate rotations across 10 years of observed weather data.
2. **Transparent Scoring**: Multi-criteria weighted scoring (Water, Soil Health, Resilience, Margin, Pest Break).
3. **Living Underground**: Procedural depiction of soil moisture, organic matter, and root systems based on calculated values.
